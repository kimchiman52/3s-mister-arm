steward-fu/sdl2 (mmiyoo backend reference; pattern-only, structurally informative)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Upstream: https://github.com/steward-fu/sdl2

3S-ARM does NOT ship any code from this repository. The mmiyoo
backend was read structurally as a reference for how to drive the
SigmaStar MI_GFX hardware blitter on the Miyoo Mini Plus. The
SigmaStar BSP headers and prebuilt shared libraries vendored at
`vendor/sigmastar/inc/` and `vendor/sigmastar/lib/` were copied
verbatim from steward-fu/sdl2's `mini/inc/` and `mini/lib/`
directories.

License: LGPL-2.1, as provided by the steward-fu/sdl2 SDL2 fork
(covers the SDL2 fork itself, not the SigmaStar headers/libs in
`mini/`; see the SigmaStar MI_SDK section below for that content's
posture).

Copyright:
- Copyright (C) Steward Fu and the SDL2 contributors.

SigmaStar MI_SDK
----------------
Upstream: SigmaStar Technology Corp. BSP for the SSD202D platform,
redistributed via steward-fu/sdl2's `mini/inc/` and `mini/lib/`.

3S-ARM links at build time against:
- libmi_sys.so
- libmi_gfx.so
- libmi_common.so

(Vendored at `vendor/sigmastar/lib/` from steward-fu/sdl2 at the
pinned commit recorded in `vendor/sigmastar.UPSTREAM.md`.)

The deployed package does NOT bundle these `.so` files. The 3S-ARM
binary's NEEDED entries (`libmi_sys.so.0`, `libmi_gfx.so.0`,
`libmi_common.so.0`) resolve at runtime from the device's
`/customer/lib/`, populated by the OnionOS firmware BSP image on
internal flash. This matches the universal community deployment
pattern across public OnionOS-adjacent projects (steward-fu/sdl2,
OnionUI Sonic Mania port, XK9274/sdl2_miyoo, OOPay/sdl2, MyMinUI).

License: vendor-proprietary. The SigmaStar headers carry a
"Sigmastar Confidential Information" banner. We redistribute under
the universal community redistribution practice for OnionOS-adjacent
projects (5+ years, zero observed enforcement). Documented stance;
see `vendor/sigmastar.UPSTREAM.md` for the full posture and the
takedown-response procedure.

Copyright:
- Copyright (C) SigmaStar Technology Corp. (2018-2019).
